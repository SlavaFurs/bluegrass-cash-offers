Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Who We Are

Bluegrass Cash Offers is a family-owned cash home buying company based in Louisville, Kentucky. We purchase residential properties directly from homeowners across Kentucky and surrounding areas.

Contact: Slava Furs  |  502-294-3811  |  slava@bluegrasscashoffers.com

Information We Collect

When you submit a request for a cash offer through our website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Property condition and timeline (if provided)

We may also collect standard website analytics data (pages visited, browser type, referral source) through tools like Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to improve site performance.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Contact you personally to discuss your property and present a cash offer
  • Follow up on your inquiry via phone, text, or email
  • Complete the home purchase transaction if you choose to proceed

We will never use your information to send unrelated marketing messages or solicit you for services unrelated to the sale of your property.

We Do Not Sell Your Information

Your personal information goes directly to Bluegrass Cash Offers and is handled by our team only. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your contact information with other real estate investors, lead aggregators, or third-party marketers.

We are not a lead generation company. When you submit your property information, you are contacting us directly — not a network of buyers.

Phone & Text Message Communications (TCPA)

By submitting your information and checking the consent box on our contact form, you agree that Bluegrass Cash Offers may contact you regarding your property inquiry by:

  • Phone call (including calls placed using automated dialing technology)
  • Text/SMS message (including automated messages)

Consent is not a condition of any purchase or sale. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out of text messages: Reply STOP at any time. To opt out of phone calls, simply tell us when we call, or email us at slava@bluegrasscashoffers.com.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate our business:

  • Formspree — receives form submissions and forwards them to our email
  • XLeads / Realeflow — our CRM system where lead information is stored
  • Google Analytics & Google Ads — website traffic and advertising analytics
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymous session recording to improve website usability

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may not use it for their own purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to manage your property inquiry and any resulting transaction. If you would like your information removed from our records, contact us at slava@bluegrasscashoffers.com and we will process your request promptly.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies primarily for analytics purposes (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity). These cookies do not identify you personally and are used only to understand how visitors interact with our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
